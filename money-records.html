Money records

Every money action leaves a record in the contract's thread. A record says what was sent, by whom, through which rail and in what state. Nothing is removed from the record. A correction is a new record that points at the old one.

Record fields

FieldMeaning
idThe action's identity, unique in its thread
principalThe person or agent that sent the action
railACH, RTP, wire or card
amountThe amount and currency, in minor units
statesent, confirmed, rejected or unknown
timeWhen the record was written, in UTC

Reading a state

  • sent: the action left Keystone. The rail has not yet answered.
  • confirmed: the rail or the bank confirmed the payment.
  • rejected: the rail or the bank refused the payment. The reason is in the record.
  • unknown: the answer was lost. Keystone does not assume success or failure. Check the bank statement, then record what you found.

Ledger entries

The books close each day. An entry is classified as it posts. A period close report lists every adjustment with its reason, and each adjustment links to the entry it changes.

Amounts are shown in tabular figures so that columns line up. Totals are computed from the entries, never typed in.
