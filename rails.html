Payment rails

Keystone moves money on three rails. Pick the rail that fits the timing and the size of the payment. The rail sets the speed, and it sets how long a payment can stay unknown.

The three rails

RailUse it forTiming
ACHScheduled transfers, payroll, recurring billsBatched; settles in business days
RTPImmediate payments between participating banksSettles in seconds when the receiving bank accepts
WireLarge sums and cross-border paymentsSame business day, with a cut-off time

What to check before you send

  1. The grant covers this account, this amount and this period.
  2. The rail is the one the payment needs. Keystone does not switch rails on its own.
  3. The payee details match the contract. A change to payee details is a new action.

When a payment is unknown

A payment stays unknown until the rail or the bank answers. Keystone does not resend it. An agent that decides to retry must record that decision as its own action, under its own grant.

Keystone publishes no rail uptime figures. Check the rail's own status page for current service.
