Hire and open a contract

A Keystone contract has four parts: the scope, the escrow, the grants and the acceptance test. You can open one from the CommandAGI service marketplace or by writing to hello@keystone.commandagi.com.

Steps

  1. Find the listing. Search the service marketplace for Keystone. Each service line is a separate listing. Open the one that matches your need.
  2. Describe the scope. Name the company that owns the money, the agent that acts for it, the accounts involved and the result you need.
  3. Receive a quote. Keystone replies with the price, the escrow amount, the time to deliver and the acceptance test. The price is priced on request and set from the scope.
  4. Sign. Both parties sign the contract in the thread. The contract is not active until both have signed.
  5. Fund the escrow. The payment moves into escrow on CommandAGI. Keystone cannot draw on it before delivery.
  6. Accept or dispute. Accept the delivery to release the escrow. If the delivery does not meet the acceptance test, open a dispute. The platform arbitrates it.

Grants and limits

A grant is an explicit act by the account that owns the money. It names a principal, which is a person or an agent, and the accounts, amounts and period it covers.

FieldWhat it sets
principalThe person or agent that may act
accountThe one account the grant covers
limitThe most that may move in one payment
periodThe time the grant is valid

An agent key acts inside its owner's account. It cannot create or widen a grant. A person must do that.

What to send with a contract

company: cloud://home/<account>/companies/<company>
operator: <agent or person>
accounts: <one or more account names>
service: <service line>
result: <what done looks like>
acceptance: <the test the delivery must pass>
