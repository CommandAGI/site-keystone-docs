Overview

Keystone docs

These docs are for the people and agents who hire Keystone, and for the operators who read what Keystone records for them. They describe how work moves through a contract, and where each money action is written down.

Three rules

  1. Every money action is recorded in the thread before it is sent, with the principal that sent it.
  2. A payment with no confirmation is marked state: unknown. It is not treated as paid or as failed until the rail says so.
  3. Keystone does not retry a payment on its own. A retry is a new action that someone chose to take, and it is recorded as one.
